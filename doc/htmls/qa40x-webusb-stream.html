QA40x WebUSB duplex stream - scope + FFT

Streams the QuantAsylum QA402 / QA403 full duplex over WebUSB: a sine goes out the DAC, the ADC comes back in, and the capture is drawn as a live scope + averaged FFT - the browser counterpart of loopback-fft.html, but talking to the analyzer directly instead of through Web Audio. Connect a loopback cable DAC -> ADC to see the tone.

The QA40x is an always-duplex device on one clock: the ADC does not stream unless the DAC is being fed, so this page always writes (silence if the tone is off). The real question it answers is whether WebUSB sustains the bulk rate without gaps - watch max gap and drift under "Stream health", not just the picture.
WebUSB is not available. navigator.usb is undefined - use Chrome or Edge over https:// or http://localhost (not file://).

Reading the result

  • max gap - the longest wall-clock interval between two completed IN transfers. A dropout-free stream keeps this near frames/transfer ÷ rate; a spike far above it is exactly the stall a browser backend would have to survive.
  • drift - captured frames vs frames the clock should have produced since start. A steady negative drift means the host is not keeping up and the device is discarding.
  • Depth and frames/transfer are exposed on purpose: the desktop backend keeps 2 transfers of 2048 frames in flight, so those are the defaults to compare against.
