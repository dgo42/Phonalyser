== [en] English ==
Phonalyser is a precision audio measurement workbench for the desktop. It combines a flexible signal generator (sine, dual-tone, sweeps and noise), a fast GPU-accelerated oscilloscope, a high-resolution FFT spectrum analyser with THD and IMD distortion readouts, and frequency-response sweeps. Bit-exact capture and playback (WASAPI / WDM-KS) keep every measurement accurate and repeatable — ideal for audio engineers, hardware developers and hi-fi enthusiasts.
